Privacy Policy – Cheta Memory Assistant

Cheta (“we”, “our”, or “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains how your data is handled when you use the Cheta Memory Assistant mobile app.

1. Data We Collect

Cheta stores the photos, notes, and voice recordings you create in the app. These are used solely to help you recall saved items. We do not collect or share personal identifiers or analytics data.

2. Data Usage

Your saved content is processed locally and securely, and is never sold or shared with third parties.

3. Security

All data is encrypted and stored privately. Uninstalling the app deletes all locally stored data.

4. Contact

Questions? Email: support@cheta.me
